Significance of Mahakaal

Shree Mahakaleshwar Temple in Ujjain is one of the twelve Jyotirlingas, the most sacred abodes of Lord Shiva. It is the only Swayambhu Jyotirlinga, deriving its power from within itself rather than being traditionally installed.

The temple is famous for its unique Bhasm Aarti, a daily ritual performed at dawn where the deity is worshipped with sacred ash, symbolizing the mastery over time and death.
